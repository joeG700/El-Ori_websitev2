Frequently asked

A few helpful details before you place your order.

Where do you deliver?

We deliver across Ghana in 1–3 days, with same-day delivery in select Accra locations. We confirm the delivery fee before dispatch.

How can I pay?

Orders are confirmed through WhatsApp. Payment is handled through the shop's Mobile Money process or on delivery. We never collect card details on this website.

How do I care for my jewelry?

Wipe each piece with the soft cloth included in your box and store it separately to help avoid scratches.
